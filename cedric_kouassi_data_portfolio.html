Distribution of Song Popularity

Right-Skewed density of tracks by popularity score (0-100)

Observation: The distribution is heavily right-skewed. The median popularity score is only 2.00, indicating that the vast majority of released songs stay relatively unknown, while only a select fraction achieve virality.

Genre Insights Analysis

Comparing occurrence counts vs. popularity metrics

Key Takeaway: Ambient and relaxation genres (background piano, calming instrumental) dominate song quantity, but mainstream urban genres (Canadian Pop, Rap, Trap, Reggaeton) achieve the highest average popularity score (~95+).

Number of Songs Released Over Time

Annual release trend trajectory (2017 - 2020)

Insight: Song releases remained steady from 2017 to 2019 (averaging ~2.4M per year), followed by a noticeable decline in 2020, likely attributable to COVID-19 pandemic disruptions in studio production and tour schedules.

Artists Most Frequently in Charts

Total accumulated days across global Top 200 charts

Dominance: Ed Sheeran stands far ahead with over 218,000 chart appearances, followed by Post Malone (~110k) and Billie Eilish (~103k), confirming extreme market concentration at the top.

Artist Followers vs. Song Popularity

Scatter plot & correlation analysis (r = 0.171)

Can music be reduced to numbers?

Cedric's analysis evaluated whether follower counts guarantee song popularity. The calculated correlation coefficient is only 0.1711.

• High Dispersion: Artists with up to 80 Million followers often release tracks with modest popularity scores.

• Breakthrough Potential: New artists with low follower counts frequently score viral hits through algorithms, TikTok trends, and playlist placements.

Predictive AI & Modeling

Machine Learning Pipeline & Performance

Leveraging Random Forest Regression to predict popularity metrics and K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) to classify song impact levels.

Regression Model

Random Forest

R² = 0.763
RMSE = 8.66 points

Trained with 100 decision trees (`ntree = 100`) on an 80/20 train-test split using features including numeric genre encoding (`genres_num`) and artist followers.

Evaluation: The Random Forest model captures ~76.3% of the variance in track popularity. The RMSE of 8.66 represents a moderate margin of error across a 0-100 scale, showing strong overall predictive power.
Classification Model

KNN (Impact vs Non-Impact)

Accuracy (k=5)

Defines Impact tracks as songs with `popularity >= 50`. Classifies track performance using K-Nearest Neighbors ($k=5$).

Data Merging & Temporal Limits

Attempting to analyze how popular music perception changed over time encountered dataset schema challenges when joining tracks and charts datasets on track names.

Empty Merged Result

Renaming `artist` to `name` created key collisions due to variations in featured artist formats across databases.

Highlights the crucial importance of unique URI key joins in real-world data science.
